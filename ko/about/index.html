About

I’m Nam (NamVS), an embedded software engineer in Ho Chi Minh City. For the last 5+ years I have worked on telematics control units: the box in a connected car that holds its modem and links it to the cloud.

My work comes down to one question: can this car be updated, diagnosed and trusted after it leaves the factory?

What I do

  • Over-the-air updates. I own firmware-update features end to end, from design to the update agent on the device to the simulators and test suites behind it. The latest one updates 30+ ECUs across 4 CAN networks.
  • Diagnostics. I build UDS services, end-of-line checks for the production line, and secured diagnostic access.
  • Secure connectivity. I work on vehicle provisioning, device authentication and the crypto behind vehicle-to-cloud links.

Most days that means C/C++ and Python on Embedded Linux or Android Automotive, talking to AUTOSAR ECUs over CAN, SOME/IP and DoIP.

Experience

FPT Software, Embedded Software Engineer · 2022 – now
Component owner for FOTA and vehicle provisioning on telematics programs for international automotive OEMs, including a platform migration from Embedded Linux to Android Automotive. I also mentor junior engineers.

Bosch Global Software Technologies, Embedded Software Engineer · 2021 – 2022
AUTOSAR Classic diagnostics and failure management for automotive sensor ECUs.

Background

  • B.Eng. in Mechatronics, HCMC University of Technology and Education (2021)
  • ISO/SAE 21434 Automotive Cybersecurity Engineering, Level 1 (2026)

Why this blog

I write down what I learn about embedded Linux, automotive protocols and security, mostly so I understand it better. If it helps you too, even better.
