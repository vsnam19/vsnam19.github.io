Hello, I'm NamVS▍

I build the software inside connected devices that lets them update over the air, report their own faults and stay trusted on the way to the cloud. Today that means cars: telematics units, from Embedded Linux and Android Automotive down to AUTOSAR ECUs.

  • › embedded software engineer
  • › connected devices
  • › software-defined vehicles

01 · ./ota

Over-the-air updates

Component owner for firmware updates end to end: design, the update agent on the telematics unit, and the simulators and tests that prove a release is safe. Shipped across 30+ ECUs on 4 CAN networks.

02 · ./diagnostics

Diagnostics

UDS services, end-of-line checks on the production line and secured diagnostic access, so a car can tell engineers what is wrong with it.

03 · ./security

Secure connectivity

Vehicle provisioning, device authentication and the crypto behind vehicle-to-cloud links. Currently studying ISO/SAE 21434.
